FAQs

A few common questions that make the enquiry process quicker and easier.

What’s the quickest way to get a quote?

WhatsApp is the quickest option, especially if you can send a short description and a few photos of the job.

Can I send over a list of small jobs?

Yes — if you have a few jobs that need doing around the house, send them over together and Michael can advise on the best way to handle them.

Do you only cover Castle Bromwich?

Castle Bromwich is the main area, but nearby locations are often covered too. If you’re slightly outside the listed areas, it’s still worth asking.

What should I include in my message?

A short description, your location and any useful photos are ideal. That makes it much easier to understand the job and provide a quote.
